Z₂ Lattice Gauge Theory (Ising Model)

Edges represent Z₂ spins (thin = 0, thick = 1). Each Monte Carlo step flips all four edges around a random plaquette, accepted with probability 1/(1+exp(ΔE/T)).

The critical temperture is 2.27. Try quenching above and below this temperature to see the different phases!
