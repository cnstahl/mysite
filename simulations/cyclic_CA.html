Cyclic Cellular Automata

Cyclic cellular automaton (CCA) on a 2D lattice. Each cell can be in one of several states (colors), and the state of each cell updates based on the states of its neighbors in a cyclic manner. The sliders allow you to adjust the system size, number of colors, temperature, and drive rate. Experiment with different settings to observe how the patterns evolve over time!
