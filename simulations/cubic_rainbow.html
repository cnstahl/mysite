Rainbow Gauge Theory (Membrane Model)

$\beta H = - h \sum_{e} (\sigma_e > 0)$

A 2d slice through a cubic lattice of the rainbow membrane model. Each Monte Carlo step flips all six faces around a random cube, accepted with the Glauber probability 1/(1+exp(ΔE/T)).
